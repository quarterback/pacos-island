# After action record: 32 bit art

**Date:** November 17, 2026
**Accountable human:** Ron Bronson, game owner

## 1. Intent

The owner compared 16 bit, 32 bit and photo based mockups of the boat scene. He found 16 bit too hard to make out and chose the drawn 32 bit look, built into the repo.

## 2. Action

- **Art:** `js/art.js` was rewritten. The art is still drawn in code, now with canvas shapes and gradients in place of single pixel fills. Paco, the panga, the ATV and the diver have smooth shading and a soft dark rim. The coast, the island climb and the dive were redrawn with gradient skies and water, mist on the far hills, a sun with glow, a lit lighthouse lamp, waterfall mist, light rays and glowing plankton in the canyon.
- **Stage:** the scene went from 384 × 192 to 480 × 240. Stop spacing, sprite offsets and the camera in `js/scene.js` were scaled to match. Scores, stops and depth marks are unchanged.
- **Sharpness:** the Pixi stage now renders at three times its size and CSS scales it with normal smoothing. The `image-rendering: pixelated` rule was removed, since 480 art pixels across a phone screen would land on uneven device pixels.
- **Share card:** `tools/og.html` was updated for the new strip size and `icons/og.png` was rebuilt from the new art.
- **Docs:** SPEC.md, README.md and the art rule in AGENTS.md describe the new look.

## 3. Checks

- `node tools/check.mjs`: 218 questions, 21 days, 0 problems.
- The title page loaded in headless Chrome with the scene and all three trip icons.
- The Pixi scene was screenshotted at nine points: three scores on each trip, including the first and last stop of the boat and ATV trips and the canyon floor.
- No copy changed, so the editorial checks were not run.

## 4. Gaps

- The art was reviewed in screenshots at 480 pixels wide and at phone width on the title page. It has not been checked on a physical phone.
- Paco keeps his bright green. The owner shared photos of a real iguana with an olive body, orange legs and a grey head; that recolor was mocked up but not applied.
- The owner's bay photos informed nothing in this build. A photo backdrop version was mocked up and not chosen.
